# 🌟 Mood Tracker 🌟

A beautiful and interactive React app to track your daily moods with emojis, colors, and history.

## Features

- **Colorful Mood Selection**: Choose from Happy 😊, Sad 😢, Angry 😠, Excited 🤩, and Calm 😌 with vibrant colors
- **Real-time Updates**: See your current mood displayed instantly
- **Mood History**: Track your mood over time with timestamps
- **Persistent Storage**: Your mood history is saved locally
- **Modern UI**: Gradient backgrounds, smooth animations, and responsive design
- **Easy to Use**: Simple interface with hover effects and clear buttons

## Getting Started

1. Install dependencies: `npm install`
2. Run the app: `npm run dev`

This will start the development server. Open your browser to the URL shown in the terminal (usually http://localhost:5173).

## How to Use

1. Click on a mood button to select your current feeling
2. View your current mood at the top
3. Check your mood history below
4. Clear history anytime with the "Clear History" button

Enjoy tracking your moods and gaining insights into your emotional patterns!
