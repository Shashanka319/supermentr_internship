Top Destinations in Karnataka

Hampi

Explore the UNESCO World Heritage site with ancient temples, ruins, and boulder-strewn landscapes that tell tales of the Vijayanagara Empire.

Mysore Palace

Visit the magnificent Mysore Palace, a stunning example of Indo-Saracenic architecture and home to the Wodeyar dynasty.

Coorg

Known as the Scotland of India, Coorg offers misty hills, coffee plantations, and adventure activities in a serene environment.

Bengaluru

Experience the vibrant capital city with its gardens, tech parks, modern malls, and a perfect blend of tradition and innovation.
